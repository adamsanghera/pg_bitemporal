The Late Letter

You've just started as a clerk in a bank's records office. Customers move, letters arrive late, and colleagues make mistakes. Your job is to keep an address book that can always answer two questions: where did they live? and when did we know?

    Every answer and every write in this game goes to real pg_bitemporal tables in your local Postgres.
